## 5. CONTENT ENGINE — 4 Repeatable Formats

Rotate these so the channel never feels templated (this is what keeps you monetizable):

| Format | Title pattern | Example | Length |
|--------|--------------|---------|--------|
| 🦁 **Predator Decision** | Why X didn't attack Y | *Why Predators Ignore Sleeping Prey* | 6–8 min |
| 🏃 **The Great Escape** | X vs Y: the 12-hour chase | *Zebra Escapes 3 Lions in the Dead of Night* | 8–10 min |
| 🔬 **Hidden Science** | The secret mechanism behind X | *The Secret Language of Elephants' Feet* | 5–7 min |
| 💀 **Last Stand** | One animal's final struggle | *The Loneliest Wolf's Last Winter* | 7–9 min |

**Video anatomy (every film):**
```
0:00–0:15  HOOK — drop the viewer mid-crisis. Open a loop ("but what the
           lions didn't know would change everything").
0:15–1:00  SETUP — who, where, what's at stake. Plant 1 fact that
           becomes the payoff.
1:00–70%   ESCALATION — 3 rising beats, each worse than the last.
70–90%     CLIMAX — the moment. Slow motion. Silence. Then sound.
90–100%    PAYOFF — answer the title's question with the real science.
           Close the loop. CTA (1 line, not begging).
```

**Retention 2.0 rules (V2 — apply to every script):**
- **Re-hook every 30–40s** — a question, a visual shift, or a "but". Scripted, not improvised.
- **Exactly ONE open loop** per film (opened in the hook, closed in the last 10%). Zero = boring; two = confusing.
- **Alternate shot scale** every scene (wide → macro → medium) to reset attention.
- **Plant one debate question** mid-film — it becomes the pinned comment and fuels hour-1 comments.
- **Series numbering** — end on "Rule #N" so playlists binge and sessions continue.

## 11. SURVIVAL RULES ⚠️ (read twice — this is what kills copycats)

1. **Disclose synthetic media.** For every realistic AI video, tick YouTube's **"Altered content"** disclosure in upload settings. Realistic fake animals without disclosure = strikes risk.
2. **Never claim fake footage is real.** Don't title things "REAL footage of…" and don't fabricate "news". AI wildlife channels get away with drama, not deception.
3. **Beat the "inauthentic content" policy** (YouTube demonetizes mass-produced, template AI content — enforcement got aggressive through 2026):
   - ✅ Original scripts with real, verified science
   - ✅ Varied formats (your 4 formats), recurring characters, series
   - ✅ Human edit pass on every script + custom thumbnails
   - ❌ Never: same template, same structure, copy-paste prompts, daily spam uploads
4. **Never copy a video.** Copy the *format*, change the story, species, and angle. The reference video itself says: make your own unique videos.
5. **One niche. One platform. One year.** The single most repeated advice in the live class. No jumping to Instagram/TikTok until YouTube is winning.
6. **Win the trust war — 2026's decisive battleground.** Viewers are actively blocking AI animal channels over *deception* (fake rescues, unnatural behaviors, fake "real" footage). Never publish those. Make transparency the brand: **"Every frame is AI — every fact is real"** in the trailer, **"AI visuals · real science"** tag on every Short, disclosure on every upload. Slop channels get buried; the transparent science channel inherits their audience.
